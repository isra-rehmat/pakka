Pakka Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Pakka is a small habit tracker for a private circle of family and friends. It is made by Okasha for personal, non-commercial use. This page explains what the app stores and who can see it.

What we collect

  • From Google sign in: your name, email address and profile picture link. We only ask Google for basic profile information.
  • What you add in the app: your display name, avatar, habits, check-ins, photo proofs, chat messages, pokes, cheers, Saza Cards, achievements and XP.

Who can see your data

  • Only people in your groups and your accountability partners can see your profile, habits, check-ins, photo proofs and Saza Cards.
  • Group chat messages are only visible to members of that group.
  • Your email address is never shown to other users.
  • Photo proofs are stored with random, unguessable links. Anyone who has a photo's exact link can open it, so only upload photos you are comfortable sharing with your circle.

How it is stored

Data is stored with Supabase (database, login and photo storage) and the app is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not sell your data, show ads, or share it with anyone else.

Deleting your data

You can leave groups and archive habits inside the app. To delete your whole account and all your data, ask the person who invited you (the group admin) or contact Okasha directly, and it will be removed.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated.
